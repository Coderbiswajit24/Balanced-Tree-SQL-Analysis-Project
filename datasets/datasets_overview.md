##### **✅Overview**

This file contains all the main data tables  used inside  the Balanced Tree Clothing Co. SQL Analysis project . These tables store information about products, sales transactions.



###### ✅Table Descriptions:



1\. product\_details (👖):

Purpose: Contains complete information about every product sold by Balanced Tree Clothing Company.

What it stores:



Product names and IDs

Prices for each product

Category information (Mens or Womens)

Segment information (Jeans, Jacket, Shirt, Socks)

Style details for each product



Business Use: This table helps us understand what products we sell, how they're organized, and their pricing structure.



2\. sales (🛒):

Purpose: Records every single transaction and purchase made by customers.

What it stores:



Transaction IDs (unique order numbers)

Product IDs (what was purchased)

Quantity sold (how many items)

Price at time of sale

Discount percentage applied

Member status (whether customer is a loyalty member)

Transaction date and time



Business Use: This is the main table for analyzing sales performance, revenue, and customer behavior.
